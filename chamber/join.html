NP Membership

Free

For nonprofit organizations. Includes a directory listing, community event invitations, and one networking meeting each month.

Bronze Membership

ARS 30,000 per year

Includes a directory listing, monthly networking meetings, two training workshops per year, and a 5% discount on chamber events.

Silver Membership

ARS 60,000 per year

Includes all Bronze benefits, four training workshops per year, eligibility for rotating home page spotlights, and a 10% discount on chamber events.

Gold Membership

ARS 100,000 per year

Includes all Silver benefits, monthly training workshops, one dedicated newsletter feature each quarter, and a 20% discount on chamber events.
